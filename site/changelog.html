changelog

What's new

Every nurb release, newest first. Install once, then nurb update keeps you current.

v0.12.0August 4, 2026
  • newDesign limits. A part can now refuse a configuration it knows can't work, like a hole narrower than the tool it holds. Instead of an error, the viewer shows a plain message, marks the slider that went too far, and keeps the last good shape on screen.
  • improvedThe Mac app checks for updates every six hours and downloads them in the background, so updating is one click when the prompt appears. A Check for Updates item in the app menu answers on demand either way.
  • fixedClicking a part in the Mac app's sidebar now switches the preview. It used to keep showing the previous part.
v0.11.0August 4, 2026
  • newThe desktop app. A Mac app that puts your projects, your AI, and the live viewer in one window. Chat with Claude, Codex, Cursor, or Grok on the left and watch the part change on the right. It sets everything up on first launch and updates itself with one click.
  • newInspection reports. Your AI can now show you exactly where a problem lives: each finding is drawn on the face it belongs to, parts can be cut open to see inside, and a verify run bundles the full checklist with rendered stills.
  • newThe model leaderboard. A public benchmark scores AI models on designing real printable parts, so you can see how the model you talk to stacks up.
  • improvedThe viewer draws your printer's actual bed and centers the part on it, so what you see is what will sit on the plate.
  • improvedYour AI looks up published dimensions itself, like a battery size or a rail spacing, and only asks you to measure what can't be found.
  • improvedTell nurb about your printer once and every project on your machine knows it. Your AI records it for you and checks there before asking again.
  • improvedExports write just the STL you print from by default. Other formats are one ask away, and a stale file sitting next to a fresh export gets flagged instead of looking current.
v0.10.0August 2, 2026
  • newRounded rims. The top of a wall that rises and falls can now be rounded over in one step. Ask for it by name when you want the softer edge; everything else still gets a chamfer by default.
  • newScrew pockets. A recessed pocket for a screw head can print facing the bed. The hole above it used to begin on thin air, and now two thin throwaway layers carry it, which the screw pushes straight through.
  • newA new check warns when a part has a hole rim floating over an open pocket, the failure the overhang rule stays quiet about.
  • improvedExported STLs are meshed at printing scale, so a large tray is a fraction of the file it used to be, and every export reports its size and triangle count.
  • improvedA part too long for your bed now passes if it fits across the diagonal, which is how you would print it anyway.
  • improvedThe viewer says when a part is rebuilding and counts the seconds, and sliders no longer snap back to old values or rebuild on every notch of a drag.
  • improvedA corner axis marker keeps orientation readable while you orbit, and finding pins can be hidden to see the geometry underneath them.
  • fixedThe viewer connects when nurb is served over HTTPS behind a proxy, instead of hanging at connecting.
  • fixedStarting the viewer falls back to another port instead of refusing to open when one is already running.
v0.9.0July 31, 2026
  • newDiagonal printing. A part that prints better tilted can now be stood on its corner, with a small flat facet cut where it meets the bed. It's offered as a variant with a plain note about the trade-off, never forced, since only you know whether that corner shows.
  • newTall diagonal stances grow break-away fins: thin blades that steady the lean during the print and snap off after.
  • newA new floating check fails any region whose first layer would print on thin air, something the overhang check can't see.
  • improvedHold the pointer over a slider's name to see what that parameter does.
v0.8.0July 30, 2026
  • improvedSliders are named in your words. Call it a lip when you describe the part and the slider says lip, not something technical.
  • improvedYour AI asks for the permissions it needs once, up front, instead of stopping to ask in the middle of the first build.
  • fixedThree viewer fixes: the section cut spans the whole part, a stale camera bug is gone, and the checks panel no longer blinks between rebuilds.
v0.7.0July 29, 2026
  • fixedWhen your browser can't do 3D, the viewer says so and explains what to try, instead of showing a blank page.
  • improvednurb notices when your installed skill is older than the tool and says how to bring it current.
v0.6.1July 29, 2026
  • newExporting an assembly downloads one zip containing each of its parts.
v0.6.0July 29, 2026
  • newAssemblies. Put parts together and check the motion between them, so a lid that would collide with its hinge gets caught before you print it.
  • newnurb.dev, with a live demo you can drag, a one-line installer, and nurb update to upgrade everything in one go.
  • fixedA typed slider value now arrives exactly as typed, Chrome no longer hangs while connecting to the viewer, and the viewer never restores an old camera onto an empty scene.
v0.5.0July 28, 2026
  • newVariants. Named configurations of a part you can flip between in the viewer, each with a link that opens exactly that configuration.
  • improvedYour AI starts the viewer at the beginning of every session and keeps its link in every reply.
v0.4.1July 28, 2026
  • improvedA viewer design pass: a cleaner toolbar and clearer icons on the printability checks.
v0.4.0July 28, 2026
  • newThe viewer's update badge upgrades nurb when you click it.
  • newA double-clickable launcher that opens the viewer without touching a terminal.
  • newPrinter profiles can choose which file formats an export produces.
v0.3.0July 28, 2026
  • newYour AI can now inspect a part the way the print checks see it, with every finding tied to the exact face it's about.
  • improvedWhen a chamfer won't fit, the error names the real cause and the edges involved, not a cryptic kernel code.

  • newThe viewer shows its version and quietly mentions when a newer one exists.
